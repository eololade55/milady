Milady style is inspired by the subcultural fashion tribes of Y2K Japan.

Details:

  • Avatar-first design: Illustrated to ensure strong social media profile aesthetics with an emphasis on fashion.
  • Chain roll function: Randomized cosmetics are organized into different style themes, with a chance of the consecutive generation sticking to the same theme, designed to organically produce aesthetic coherency.
  • Graded for Drip: Miladys have ranked badges on a variety of weighted metrics, adding additional layers to a speculative market beyond objective trait scarcity.
  • Neochibi Aesthetic: All art in a unique childlike take on anime illustration.
  • Tempo chain: 10,000 supply. Mint price 5 pathUSD.

Style Themes

Tempo Miladys generate a variety of accessories, but the two primary cosmetics — Hat & Shirt — are each divided into five major style themes inspired by Tokyo fashion tribes:

Gyaru
Prep
Lolita
Hypebeast
Harajuku

The bottom border of a Milady card is defined by its theme(s). A Milady whose Hat & Shirt cosmetic share the same theme receives +5 on their Drip Score.

Drip Grade

Tempo Miladys each have a drip score rating their overall stylishness, with each cosmetic scored on its rarity, then weighted against the prominence of its slot, with a multiplier applied for theme or costume complete badges. Drip scores tie value to aesthetics over pure trait scarcity.

Miladys are given a letter grading for their drip score (D, C, B, A, S, SS) to help categorize an individual Milady's quality.
